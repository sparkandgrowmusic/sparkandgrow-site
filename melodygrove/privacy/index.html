Melody Grove · Privacy policy

Privacy

Effective November 27, 2026 · Spark & Grow Music LLC

Melody Grove does not collect, share or sell any personal information, from anyone, of any age. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

Melody Grove is an app for iPhone made by Spark & Grow Music LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information about you.

What stays on your device

The app keeps the scene you are in, the choices you make in each place, and your progress, such as the felt seeds you have earned and the things you have chosen at the shop, in its own storage on your device, so they are there next time you open it. This information never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Seeds and the shop

The felt seeds in Melody Grove are earned only by playing. They cannot be bought, and nothing in the app costs real money.

Crash reports through Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash and performance reports. You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Microphone, camera and location

Melody Grove does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

Children

Melody Grove does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
